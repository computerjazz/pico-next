# The Static That Wouldn't Die

For weeks, Shortwave had an intermittent static burst whenever playback got
interrupted mid-message. My first theory was a buffer underrun in the I2S
DMA — classic audio glitch territory. It wasn't that.

<Image
  src={pinout}
  alt="I2S pinout diagram showing the mic and speaker sharing BCLK and WS lines"
/>

The mic (INMP441) and the speaker's I2S DAC were wired to the **same BCLK
and WS lines**, just on separate data pins. That's normally fine for
simultaneous read/write — until playback gets interrupted and the RX task
is torn down while the clock lines are still toggling mid-word-select
cycle. The DAC would latch a partial frame, and out came static.

Here's the scope trace once I isolated it — you can see the WS line glitch
right at the interruption point:

The fix was ensuring both I2S peripherals fully drain and reset their DMA
buffers before either task restarts, rather than just killing the FreeRTOS
task outright.
